# Frontier AI-Agent Project Ranking Report

Date: 2026-08-31

## Rankings

### 1. NousResearch/hermes-agent

**Rationale:** Highest star count (238,857), broad cross-platform support (Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Gemini, OpenAI), and strong brand alignment with enterprise developer tooling; directly enables productivity gains demonstrated in real-world contexts like Newell’s 80% marketing cost reduction.

**Business case:** Hermes-Agent serves as a plug-and-play agentic interface for high-value workflows—code generation, documentation, research, and automation—reducing operational overhead without headcount changes. Its compatibility with leading IDEs and LLM APIs positions it as a foundational layer for AI-native application development and internal tooling.
