**Rationale:** With 248,827 stars and active daily updates (last updated 2026-09-25), Hermes-Agent is the most widely adopted open-source agent framework that directly enables enterprise-ready, user-growing personal AI agents. Its deep integration with Anthropic, Claude Code, Codex, and OpenAI ecosystems — combined with strong alignment to the 'AI assistant consolidation' trend highlighted by Microsoft's Copilot reboot — positions it as the de facto standard for production agentic workflows.

**Business case:** Hermes-Agent serves as a scalable foundation for B2B SaaS vendors building vertical AI assistants (e.g., legal, healthcare, finance) and enables rapid customization via skills, memory, and MCP-compliant tooling. Its adoption signals market readiness for interoperable, user-adaptive agents — a strategic hedge against platform lock-in amid hyperscaler infrastructure concentration.
